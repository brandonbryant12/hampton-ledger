# Design direction

A civic field guide inspired by Hampton’s tidal landscape and the precision of a financial ledger. Deep harbor #102f3a, Atlantic #21556a, sea glass #b9dbc9, paper #f7f7f0, ink #18363d, muted slate #53666b. Georgia carries display headlines; a system humanist sans carries navigation and financial detail.

The defining graphic is a custom tidal contour illustration which becomes an interactive allocation chart in the tax explorer. Left-aligned copy, generous spacing, quiet surfaces, narrow readable text. Evidence and reporting years appear beside claims. Avoid generic KPI-card grids, excessive labels, and decorative badges.

Desktop: compact brand/navigation, spacious two-column cover, horizontal evidence summary, integrated tax explorer, research/library views. Mobile: single column, accessible persistent navigation, touch-friendly filters. Respect reduced motion and keyboard users.

Review: this direction connects Hampton’s coast to civic accounting. The visual emphasis belongs to the tax allocation experience; typography and dividers stay restrained.
