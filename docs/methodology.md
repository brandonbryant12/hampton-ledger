# Evidence and calculation methodology

Hampton Ledger is an independent civic transparency project, not an official tax service or a professional financial audit.

## Published scope

- Residential illustrations use Hampton's published **2025** tax-rate table.
- Operating-finance summaries cover **2024 and 2025**, using each annual report's unaudited Finance Department narrative.
- The library includes preserved source directories, the published purchasing policy, selected recent Select Board and Budget Committee documents, and finance excerpts.
- No complete vendor register, general ledger, bank reconciliation, meeting-video transcript archive, AI findings, or verified savings are claimed.

## Taxes

Annual illustration = assessed value / 1,000 × published rate. Outside precinct: 12.61; partial precinct category 3000: 12.67; full precinct category 2000: 13.23. The full precinct addition combines the 0.56 precinct and 0.06 precinct-exempt components shown in the source table. These labels mirror the published categories; the application does not determine eligibility.

Credits, exemptions, abatements, utility classifications, and future tax rates are excluded. Displayed dollar components round independently; downloaded CSV retains cents. Component percentages may sum to 99.9% or 100.1% after rounding.

## Operating finance

Source: 2024 Annual Report, printed pages 146–148 (PDF pages 152–154), and 2025 Annual Report, printed pages 205–207 (PDF pages 211–213).

The reported operating expense totals include encumbrances/purchase orders and exclude warrant-article expenditures. They are not cash-payment totals or all-funds expenditures. The 2025 narrative separately reports warrant-article expenditures including purchase orders; the site displays that figure separately. Tax effort and other revenue are separate source measures and are not added together to imply a reconciled operating budget.

The charts in the annual report can describe budgeted categories; they are not automatically treated as actual departmental expenditures. Unspent authorization is not automatically a recurring saving. Current-year figures were unaudited when published and may subsequently change.

## Preservation

Each record includes the original URL, retrieval timestamp, SHA-256 of retrieved bytes, archive path, archive hash, format, extraction method, and period where known. Original HTML is downloadable as plain text so source scripts cannot execute on this site. PDF excerpts identify their original page ranges and retain separate hashes for the full source and the excerpt. The complete annual reports are linked but not republished; unrelated vital-record sections are excluded from the searchable collection.

HTML extraction and PDF text extraction can lose formatting. Original records take precedence over extracted text. Collection time is not publication time. Source directories do not imply their linked documents have been ingested.

## Collection and review

The collector uses an explicit official-source registry and bounded discovery of recent Select Board and Budget Committee documents. It preserves prior versions and fails visibly when a source cannot be retrieved or extracted. It does not bypass authentication, disable TLS verification, send requests to officials, or treat absent records as wrongdoing.

Scheduled collection produces a review artifact. It does not automatically change published financial figures or deploy source changes. A maintainer reviews content and privacy implications, validates evidence hashes and calculations, then commits an update.

## Findings and corrections

No AI findings are currently published. Future findings must distinguish fact, inference, unanswered question, and quantified opportunity. They must include primary evidence, the applicable policy/date, calculation, limitations, reviewer, and an opportunity to correct the record.

Report errors through the public GitHub correction template with a source link. Never post private financial, medical, bank-account, tax-identifier, or beneficiary information. Public-source availability does not eliminate the need for publication judgment.
