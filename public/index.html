Why can my bill change?

Your bill reflects both the tax rate and your assessed value. Changes in budgets, other revenues, and the town’s taxable value can all affect the rate. A lower rate alone does not mean a lower bill.

What would savings mean?

Actual tax relief depends on which public body saves money, whether the saving recurs, and how it changes the amount raised through property taxes. We will track proposed, adopted, and realized savings separately.

Our method

Trust the trail.
Check our work.

Hampton Ledger is a resident-led, open-source project. Our purpose is to make public finances understandable and help residents identify well-supported improvements.

Evidence before conclusions.

Every financial claim on this site points back to a public source. We preserve retrieved records, record their content hashes, and explain our calculations. You can inspect the same material we used.

A missing record means our evidence is incomplete. It does not, by itself, establish waste, wrongdoing, or a legal violation.

Hampton Ledger is independent of the Town of Hampton and other public bodies. It is not an official tax service or a professional financial audit.
